How to read this

Only one sender drives the W bus at a time, while one or more selected registers may listen. A receiving register loads the bus value on the clock’s rising edge. The controller-sequencer advances through T1–T6 and asserts the control signals that choose each sender and receiver. Names with a bar are active-low signals: the physical line is asserted low even though the diagram highlights it as active. Amber marks data being sent and teal marks a destination or active control path.
