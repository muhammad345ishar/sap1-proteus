SAP-1 Controller Sequencer

A 4-to-16 decoder turns the opcode into LDA, ADD, SUB, OUT and HLT lines, and the step counter gives T1–T6. An AND–OR–NOT matrix then produces the 12 control signals. Step the clock and watch each gate light up.

The 74154 outputs are active-low, so T1–T6 and LDA/ADD/SUB/OUT pass through inverters (74LS04) first. L̄I takes T̄3 straight from the decoder with no gate. With NAND only, the same logic needs 28 gates (7× 74LS00).

Opcodes: LDA 0000 · ADD 0001 · SUB 0010 · OUT 1110 · HLT 1111. Test program: LDA 9H, ADD AH, ADD BH, SUB CH, OUT, HLT with 16, 20, 24, 32 in RAM, so OUT = 28 (1CH).
