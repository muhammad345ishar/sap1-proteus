IC workbench

Each chip from your SAP-1 is wired on its own test bench, just as you would test it in Proteus: click a logic-state switch to flip an input, press CLK to give a clock pulse, and read the probes. Next to it is the circuit inside the chip, built from flip-flops, multiplexers, buffers and adders, with every internal wire coloured live. Red = 1, blue = 0, dashed grey = disconnected (Z).
